I am investigating algorithms with predictions for the Traveling Salesman Problem, where a predictor identifies a 
small (=constant size) set of subtour elimination constraints rather than directly predicting the optimal tour. 
The goal is to exploit these predictions to efficiently construct and repair low-cost 2-factors, providing provable 
guarantees that degrade gracefully with prediction error.
